About

Who I am

I'm an electrical engineer who has spent most of his career at the equipment, not behind a desk — commissioning drive systems on jack-up rigs in the Arabian Gulf, running maintenance for unionized trades in a steel mill, and now leading reliability engineering across a multi-vendor automation environment in Ontario.

My path has moved from execution toward reliability strategy: root-cause analysis on repeat failures, criticality-based maintenance planning, and the automation and instrumentation systems that keep heavy industrial equipment running. I hold a PMP and an MEng from the University of Waterloo, and I'm currently working through the technical competency documentation for a P.Eng. license with APEGA.

Outside engineering, I serve as an officer in the Canadian Army Reserve and sit on the board of a non-profit that teaches technology skills to youth and seniors — both places where discipline and teaching intersect with the same instincts I bring to industrial reliability work.
